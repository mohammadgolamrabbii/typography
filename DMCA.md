# ⚖️ Open Typography Foundation — DMCA & Copyright Compliance Policy

The **Open Typography Foundation**, curated and maintained by **Mohammad Golamrabbii**, is a non-profit, public-benefit digital heritage preservation initiative dedicated to indexing, organizing, and distributing **100% free and open-source typefaces** for every writing system on Earth.

Every font in this foundation is curated strictly under recognized permissive open-source licenses, including:
- **SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL)**
- **Apache License, Version 2.0**
- **MIT License**
- **Creative Commons Zero (CC0) / Public Domain**

We respect the intellectual property rights of type designers, independent foundries, and artists worldwide. We enforce a zero-tolerance policy against unauthorized proprietary or pirated commercial typography.

---

## 🏛️ Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) Notice & Takedown Procedure

If you are a copyright or trademark owner (or authorized to act on behalf of one) and believe in good faith that any typeface or binary hosted in our vaults infringes upon your copyright, please submit a formal takedown notice containing the following information:

1. **Identification of the copyrighted work:** The exact font family name, designer, and copyright registration number (if applicable).
2. **Identification of the infringing material:** The specific URL(s) or repository path(s) within the Open Typography Foundation where the material is located.
3. **Your contact information:** Your full legal name, company/foundry name, physical address, telephone number, and official email address.
4. **Statement of Good Faith:** A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
5. **Statement of Accuracy & Perjury:** A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notification is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner.
6. **Physical or Electronic Signature:** A physical or electronic signature of the authorized copyright holder.

## ⚡ Resolution Commitment (24–48 Hours)

Upon receipt of a valid and substantiated infringement notice, our compliance team commits to:
1. **Immediate Quarantine:** Removing or disabling public access to the identified font file within **24 to 48 hours**.
2. **Audit Verification:** Reviewing the font's metadata tables (Name Table IDs 0, 7, 13, 14: Copyright, Trademark, License Description, License URL).
3. **Permanent Removal:** Permanently purging any confirmed non-open-source asset from active mirrors and tagging release errata.

---

## 🔄 Counter-Notification Procedure

If a font contributor believes their material was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification, they may file a formal counter-notification containing proof of open-source licensing (such as an authentic OFL.txt or Apache 2.0 license signed by the original author).
